Privacy Policy

Hermes Drive Backup · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hermes Drive Backup ("the app") accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when it connects to Google Drive. The app is a private, single-user backup tool operated by its owner. It is not distributed to the public.

1. What the app accesses

The app requests access to Google Drive. It uses that access only to:

  • create and upload encrypted backup archives to a dedicated folder in the owner's Google Drive;
  • list the archives in that folder;
  • download an archive when the owner performs a restore;
  • delete archives older than the retention period.

It does not access, read, modify, or share any other files in the Google Account.

2. What the app stores

The app stores encrypted backup archives in the owner's Google Drive. The archives contain a copy of the owner's self-hosted dashboard, including its source code and configuration. The encryption key is stored on the owner's server and is never uploaded to Google Drive.

3. How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the backup and restore function described above. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or training machine learning models. It is not sold or rented.

4. Sharing

The app does not share Google user data with any third party. The only recipient is the owner's own Google Drive account. No data is sent to the app developer or any other service, except the encrypted archive that the owner chooses to store in their own Drive.

5. Retention and deletion

Archives are kept for 30 days in Google Drive and 14 days on the owner's server, after which they are deleted automatically. The owner can delete any archive at any time by removing it from the backup folder in Google Drive. Revoking the app's access in the Google Account permissions page stops all future access.

6. Security

Backups are encrypted with AES-256 before upload using rclone crypt. OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's server with file permissions restricted to the owner. The app connects over HTTPS.

7. Limited Use

Hermes Drive Backup's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project issue tracker.
