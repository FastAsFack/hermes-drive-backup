Private backup tool

Hermes Drive Backup

Encrypted, automatic backups of a self-hosted dashboard to the owner's Google Drive.

What it does

Hermes Drive Backup is a private, single-user tool. Once a day it creates an archive of a self-hosted dashboard, encrypts it, uploads it to a dedicated folder in the owner's Google Drive, and removes archives older than 30 days.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no account system, and no other user. It runs on the owner's own server and is used only by the owner.

How it uses Google Drive

The app requests access to Google Drive only to create, list, download, and delete its own backup archives in a folder it owns. It does not read, modify, or share any other files in the Google Account.

Data and privacy

Backups are encrypted before they leave the owner's server. The encryption key is stored on that server, not in Google Drive. Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
